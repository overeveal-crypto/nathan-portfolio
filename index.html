Lore

Where I study, what I build, and how the shop began.

I'm a 3rd-year BSIT student at Bulacan Agricultural State College, and I spend most of my free time digging through code. Web apps, backend systems, and tech that runs itself are the main quest.

Alongside school I run a digital marketplace. Trading game currencies and items taught me things a classroom rarely does: how to move money safely, how to keep a community store online, and why automation beats doing it by hand.

I like projects where the front end looks good and the back end holds up when real people use it.
